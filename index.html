The Framework

2 Core Axes — And Why Nobody Is Just One Letter

Your style comes from crossing your Vertical Pace with your Horizontal Focus—but none of us carry just one profile.

1. Vertical Axis: Your Pace

TOP (D & i): Fast-paced, active, assertive, bold, outspoken.
BOTTOM (C & S): Thoughtful, calm, methodical, careful, measured.

2. Horizontal Axis: Your Focus

LEFT (D & C): Questioning, logic-focused, skeptical, objective.
RIGHT (i & S): Accepting, people-focused, empathizing, receptive.

🦎 Blends & Chameleons

Nobody is 100% one letter. Most of us naturally lean toward a 2-profile blend (like CD, Di, or iS), and some of us are chameleons who can flex across 3 or all 4 profiles depending on the situation.

D
Priorities: Results • Action • Challenge

The "D" Style: Dominance

Motto: "Get to the bottom line—yesterday."
🚀 Superpower in Business & on the Board

Fearless closer and natural driver. When a closing wire is stuck at 4:45 PM or a board meeting is spinning in circles for 40 minutes, the D grabs the wheel, cuts through the BS, makes the hard call, and drives it across the finish line.

📱 How to Spot a "D" Text or Email
• "Got it. Done." (Zero emojis, sent while driving)
• "Skip the backstory—what's the bottom line?"
• "Call me." (Because typing takes 12 seconds too long)
🧨 Secret Fear & Toxic Trait Under Stress

Fears: Losing control, being taken advantage of, or moving at a snail's pace.
Under Stress: Can become blunt, impatient, and accidentally steamroll quieter board members in the name of "efficiency."

🔑 Cheat Code to Work With a "D" in 2027

Be direct, lead with the result first, and don't waste their time with 20-minute tangents. Give them ownership of big, ambitious targets—D loves a good challenge and to win big.

i
Priorities: Enthusiasm • Action • Collaboration

The "i" Style: Influence

Motto: "Let's make it epic—and order tequila shots!"
🎉 Superpower in Business & on the Board

The ultimate rainmaker, hype-machine, and connector. They walk into an AREAA mixer knowing 80% of the room on a first-name basis, sell out Gala tables over happy hour, and talk stressed-out clients off the ledge with pure charm.

📱 How to Spot an "i" Text or Phone Screen
• "OMG YESSS!! Let's do it!! 🔥🎉🍾🙌"
• Sends three 2-minute voice notes while driving on I-285
• Phone currently has 147 unread texts & 14,000 unread emails
🧨 Secret Fear & Toxic Trait Under Stress

Fears: Rejection, being ignored, or being trapped in boring routine spreadsheets.
Under Stress: Overpromises, gets scattered, wings the details, and forgets to follow through after the initial hype wears off.

🔑 Cheat Code to Work With an "i" in 2027

Match their energy, give them public recognition, let them brainstorm out loud, and pair them with a detail-oriented teammate to help lock in the follow-through.

S
Priorities: Support • Stability • Collaboration

The "S" Style: Steadiness

Motto: "No ego, no drama—we cross the finish line together."
🤝 Superpower in Business & on the Board

The glue and emotional rock of the board. While the D's and i's are making noise, the S makes sure every guest feels welcomed, every committee volunteer feels supported, and clients stay loyal for 15 years because they genuinely care.

📱 How to Spot an "S" in the Group Chat
• "Thanks so much for putting this together, guys! 😊🙏"
• "I'm good with whatever restaurant works best for everyone!"
• Quietly stays late after the event to help pack up the boxes
🧨 Secret Fear & Toxic Trait Under Stress

Fears: Sudden chaotic change, aggressive conflict, or letting teammates down.
Under Stress: Says "Yes" to way too much to keep the peace, stays silent when they disagree, and quietly holds resentment inside until they burn out.

🔑 Cheat Code to Work With an "S" in 2027

Don't mistake their silence for agreement! Ask for their opinion privately without pressuring them, give them advance notice before big pivots, and show genuine appreciation.

C
Priorities: Accuracy • Stability • Challenge

The "C" Style: Conscientiousness

Motto: "In God we trust—everyone else, bring data & receipts."
📊 Superpower in Business & on the Board

The master architect and guardian of quality. They actually read the venue contract, catch the $4,000 math error on the closing disclosure, keep the chapter budget bulletproof, and save the board from amateur-hour mistakes.

📱 How to Spot a "C" Email or Inbox
• Structured bullet points with 3 PDF attachments & timestamps
• "Before we vote, what is the exact ROI and budget impact?"
• Zero unread emails—inbox is color-coded by folder
🧨 Secret Fear & Toxic Trait Under Stress

Fears: Sloppy work, being wrong, or reckless decisions made on pure emotion.
Under Stress: Analysis paralysis, becoming overly skeptical/critical of new ideas, and holing up alone because "if I want it done right, I'll do it myself."

🔑 Cheat Code to Work With a "C" in 2027

Bring facts instead of hype, give them time to review the numbers before demanding a vote, and respect that their questions aren't personal attacks—they're protecting the board!

Our 2027 AREAA ATL Board Playbook

🛡️ Core Strength Heavy S + C

High trust, zero toxic ego, reliable follow-through & bulletproof budgets.

⚡ Watch-Out Light D + i

Don't be too polite or slow—be intentional about speed, bold hype & calling the vote.

🦎 Our Bridge Blends & Flex

Use fast drivers & flexers (Nini, Tony, Crystal, Kevin D., Kevin H.) to connect pace & precision.

🎯 1. Committee Pairing (Spark + Anchor)

D / i / CD + S / C
Signature Events & Gala: Pair Vibe & Hype (iD / Si) with Budget & Contracts (CD / CS / SC).
Sponsorships: Open doors & pitch vision (iD / CD / Di) → Lock in invoices & deliverables (S / SC / CS).

⏱️ 2. Meetings That Never Drag

4-Quadrant Protocol
24h Before (For C): Send budget numbers & agenda ahead of time.
In the Room (For S): Silence ≠ agreement! Call on quiet voices before voting.
At the Finish (For D & i): Cut circular debates, celebrate wins & call the vote.

💬 3. Group Chat & Friction Translator

Assume Positive Intent
🔴 "Got it. Let's vote." → Not bossy; D / CD protecting our clock.
🟡 "What's the ROI & contract say?" → Not killing the vibe; C / CS protecting the chapter.
🔵 "Sure, I'll take that on too." → Check S / Si bandwidth so nobody burns out silently.
